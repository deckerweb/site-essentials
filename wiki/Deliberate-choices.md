# Focused by design. Your choice by default.

**What Site Essentials deliberately leaves out – and how that helps you.**

[Deutsch](Bewusste-Entscheidungen)

Your WordPress. Your settings.

Site Essentials helps you manage your website and understand your choices. That also means deliberately leaving some tasks out. These boundaries help you decide whether the plugin fits your website. They are neither restrictions on you nor a judgement of other tools.

## Your configuration remains your decision

Site Essentials does not enable cleanup across the board or remove WordPress features just in case. You choose what fits your website. Some administrator-only notices are enabled initially; they do not change content or public visibility.

**What this gives you:** You can understand a setting before choosing to use it.

## A preset is a suggestion, not an automatic change

You can review a selected preset or configuration import as a draft before saving these settings. Separate actions, such as activating maintenance or migrating settings, have their own confirmation.

**What this gives you:** A useful starting point supports your decision instead of making it for you.

## Focused tools rather than a complete suite

Site Essentials brings selected administration tasks together. An executable code snippet manager, a form builder and a page builder are outside its product concept.

**What this gives you:** You remain free to choose specialist tools independently. Site Essentials does not have to handle every task on your website.

## Maintenance means maintenance

Maintenance mode uses HTTP 503: the website is temporarily unavailable. We deliberately do not offer a Coming Soon mode for a longer-term announcement page. That publishing workflow has different availability and search-engine requirements.

**What this gives you:** You get a tool with a clear purpose. For an announcement page, choose a solution designed for that workflow.

## Focused access rules rather than complete role management

Site Essentials provides targeted access rules for menus, widgets and the Customizer. Managing every WordPress role and capability is outside its scope. Hiding menu links also does not protect the linked content.

**What this gives you:** Simple client handovers stay simple. Use a specialist tool for a comprehensive permissions policy.

## Observable checks rather than security or legal guarantees

Website checks highlight observable conditions. They do not certify the legal quality of a legal notice or privacy policy, or the complete security of a website. Maintenance mode is not comprehensive access protection either.

**What this gives you:** You can see what was checked and which questions need a separate professional assessment.

## Cooperation rather than overriding other tools

For supported overlaps, Site Essentials respects the responsible provider, such as an active builder maintenance mode. It does not reconfigure other plugins without your decision. When migrating Falcon or Unbloater settings, you remove the previous plugins yourself.

**What this gives you:** You control your combination of plugins and the timing of a change.

## Focused media helpers rather than a second media manager

File sizes, filters and upload rules improve the existing Media Library. SVG security sanitization and replacement of existing media files are outside the scope. Use a dedicated solution for SVG sanitization.

**What this gives you:** Complex file processing receives the attention of an appropriate specialist tool.

**A clear scope. Understandable choices. Room for the tools you choose yourself.**
